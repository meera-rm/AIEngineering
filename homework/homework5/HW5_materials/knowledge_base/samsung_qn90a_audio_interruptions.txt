## Symptoms

Use this article when sound cuts out, crackles, or falls out of sync on a
Samsung QN90A while the picture remains stable. Common retrieval language
includes audio interruption, sound dropout, eARC instability, and intermittent
volume loss.

## Safe checks

Ask the customer whether the dropout occurs with internal streaming apps, an
HDMI source, or only when a soundbar is connected. Safe checks include reseating
the HDMI cable, temporarily disabling eARC for testing, switching the digital
audio output format, and testing the TV speakers directly. If the issue appears
only on one external device, support should narrow the investigation to that
source path.

## What support provides

Support interprets the results and decides whether the next step is audio
configuration review, accessory isolation, or hardware service for the audio
path. Picture flicker, backlight pulsing, and display artifacts are outside the
scope of this article.

## Limits

Do not use audio troubleshooting as evidence that the panel itself is defective.
If the customer reports flashing images, menu flicker, or brightness pulsing,
move the case to the display-troubleshooting flow instead of continuing with
speaker or eARC checks.
