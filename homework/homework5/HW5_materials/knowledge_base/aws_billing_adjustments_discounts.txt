## Overview

This article explains when support may recommend a billing adjustment, service
credit, or discount review after an AWS Management Service statement has been
examined. Customers often ask whether unapplied pricing options, revised usage
bands, or agreement-based discounts should have lowered the total. Eligibility
is determined after the billing review, not from the request alone.

## Adjustment eligibility

An adjustment may be considered when support confirms one of three conditions:
the customer was billed for a managed service task that was not executed, an
approved service credit from the same billing cycle was omitted, or the account
was mapped to the wrong rate schedule under the current agreement. Adjustments
are not issued because the customer's forecast was lower than the final usage,
because traffic volume increased unexpectedly, or because a project became more
expensive than planned.

## Discount review

Discount review applies when the customer asks whether a better pricing
mechanism should have been used. Support checks whether the account met the
usage-band threshold defined in its service agreement for the full billing
cycle, whether a scheduled promotional discount was active during that period,
and whether the customer's service package allowed bundled rate treatment across
multiple managed environments. If the conditions were not continuously met,
support explains why no discount was applied.

## What the customer provides and what support decides

The customer should provide the account number, the disputed statement period,
and any documentation that mentions the rate or discount they expected. They do
not need to calculate the corrected amount. Support verifies the agreement
terms, reviews usage against the threshold rules, and states whether the result
is no change, an adjustment recommendation, or referral to finance approval.

## Limits

Support can explain available paths and recommend an adjustment, but final
posting is performed by finance operations. Courtesy credits are not part of
the standard AWS Management Service policy. Escalate only when the billing
review confirms an internal rating error or an omitted approved credit.
