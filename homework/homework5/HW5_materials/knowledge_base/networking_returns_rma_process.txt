## Overview

This article covers return requests for networking hardware after delivery. It
is appropriate when a customer asks what happens if a router or similar product
does not meet expectations and wants to understand the support process before
ordering.

## Eligibility

Standard hardware returns are accepted within 30 calendar days of delivery when
the customer requests a return merchandise authorization, or RMA, and the unit
is returned with its original major components. Open-box returns are allowed,
but missing modules, damaged ports, or altered serial labels move the case to
manual review. Compatibility disappointment by itself does not guarantee a full
refund if the product was sold correctly to the documented specification.

## What the customer provides

The customer should provide the order number, delivery date, model, serial
number if available, and the reason for return. If the package arrived damaged,
photos of the carton and contents are helpful. The customer is not expected to
create the RMA code on their own.

## What support provides

Support confirms whether the request is inside the return window, issues the RMA
identifier, and explains where the item must be shipped. If restocking applies,
support states it before the return is finalized. Once the returned hardware is
received and passes inspection, support closes the RMA and releases the refund
or replacement outcome tied to the order type.

When the customer is asking before purchase, support can explain that the RMA
process exists without predicting the final inspection outcome in advance.
Returned hardware is checked for missing accessories, altered serial labels, and
obvious port damage before the case is completed. If inspection finds a mismatch
with the submitted return reason, support documents that difference for the
customer rather than silently reducing the outcome.

## Limits

This article does not estimate shipping transit times, provide technical sizing
advice, or approve firmware-related warranty claims. Escalate only when the
serial number does not match the shipped order or the return involves shipping
damage across multiple boxes.
