## Overview

Use this article when a customer needs a copy of a statement, invoice, payment
receipt, or remittance confirmation for AWS Management Service charges that have
already been processed. This article is about billing documents and payment
records only. It does not review whether charges were correct, predict future
usage, or assess discount eligibility.

## What the customer provides

The customer should provide the account number, the statement month or quarter,
and the document they need reissued. If they are matching a payment, support
also needs the payment date or the reference number from the remittance. The
customer is not expected to explain technical usage details for this request.

## What support provides

Support can resend archived statements, issue payment confirmations, and note
whether a charge has been marked paid, pending settlement, or reversed. If the
customer cannot locate an invoice attachment, support can confirm whether the
document was sent by portal message or by email from the finance mailbox.

## Limits

This article does not answer disputes about why a charge appeared, whether an
adjustment is available, or whether a pricing discount should have applied. Use
the billing-review process for those questions.
