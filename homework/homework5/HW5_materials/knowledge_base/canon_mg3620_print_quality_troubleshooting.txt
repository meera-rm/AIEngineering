## Symptoms

Use this article when a Canon PIXMA MG3620 produces poor print quality such as
streaks, blotches, banding, or uneven color after normal cartridge checks. The
goal is to document safe corrective steps and determine when the defect has
persisted long enough to justify warranty handling.

## Safe troubleshooting steps

Start by confirming whether the issue appears on both plain-text pages and photo
prints. Ask the customer to print a nozzle check pattern and keep the page as
evidence. If gaps or smearing appear, the customer can run one standard head
cleaning, wait five minutes, and print a second nozzle check. If the pattern is
still poor, one deep cleaning cycle is acceptable. Support should not ask the
customer to repeat cleaning over and over, because excessive cleaning consumes
ink without resolving a hardware defect.

The customer may also confirm that the cartridges are seated correctly, remove
any protective tape that was left in place, and test with fresh paper stored in
a dry location. If borderless photo output is affected but text pages are
clean, support should ask for one standard document sample and one photo sample
before moving on.

## What the customer provides

For quality cases, the customer should provide the printer serial number,
purchase date if known, the nozzle check page or clear photos of it, and a note
describing which troubleshooting steps have already been tried. If different
paper types were tested, that detail is useful because it helps rule out media
handling problems. The customer does not need to disassemble the unit or clean
internal components by hand.

## What support provides

Support reviews the samples to decide whether the problem looks like clogged
output, alignment drift, or a persistent print-path defect. When the customer
has already completed the approved cleaning and alignment checks without
improvement, support should stop further repetitive troubleshooting and move to
warranty eligibility review. Escalate sooner if the printer smears immediately
after setup, leaves ink pools, or cannot produce a readable nozzle check page.
