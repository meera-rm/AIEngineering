## Overview

This article describes when a Canon PIXMA MG3620 with unresolved print-quality
defects may qualify for an in-warranty exchange. In this corpus, the MG3620 is
covered by a 12-month limited hardware warranty starting on the purchase date.
Exchange is used when standard print-quality troubleshooting has been completed
and the device still cannot produce acceptable output.

## What the customer provides

The customer should provide proof of purchase, the serial number, current
shipping address, and either the saved nozzle check page or clear photos of the
defect. Support also needs confirmation that the customer completed the approved
cleaning and alignment steps and that the unit has not been physically damaged.
If aftermarket ink caused leakage or contamination, the case requires manual
review before exchange approval.

The customer is not expected to choose between repair and exchange. That
decision belongs to support under the product policy.

## What support provides

Support verifies the warranty date, reviews the troubleshooting record, and
confirms whether the defect qualifies for advanced exchange or standard return
and replace. For qualifying MG3620 print defects, the default path is exchange
rather than bench repair because repeated cleaning issues on this model are not
handled as field-service events. Support issues the exchange authorization,
explains packing requirements, and states whether the replacement is shipped
before or after the defective unit is scanned by the carrier.

When exchange is approved, support should tell the customer which accessories
must be returned and which items to keep, such as opened ink consumables when
the policy excludes them from the return carton. Support also provides the
expected replacement timeline and explains that the replacement unit is matched
to the original warranty term rather than starting a fresh 12-month period.

## Eligibility limits

Exchange is intended for persistent print-quality failures, not wireless setup
problems, consumable depletion, or accidental breakage. Missing proof of
purchase does not close the case, but it moves the request to manual warranty
date verification. Escalate when the serial number is unreadable, the purchase
date conflicts with the system record, or contamination from third-party ink
makes eligibility uncertain.
