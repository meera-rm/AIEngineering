## Overview

Use this article when the Canon PIXMA MG3620 cannot join Wi-Fi, disappears from
the print list, or repeatedly drops off the wireless network. It does not cover
streaking, blotches, or other print-quality defects once a page is already
printing.

## What the customer provides

Ask the customer whether the printer fails initial setup or loses connection
after working previously. Support also needs the network name, whether the router
provides separate 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands, and whether other devices can reach
the same network normally.

## What support provides

Support can guide the customer through network reset, reconnecting to the 2.4
GHz band used by this model, and confirming that the printer receives an IP
address. If the printer is visible and jobs reach it but printed pages are
defective, stop using this article and move to print-quality troubleshooting.

## Limits

Wireless recovery should not include repeated factory resets when the symptom is
actually defective output on already delivered jobs. This article does not
support exchange eligibility, ink-system diagnosis, or mechanical repair.
