## Overview

This article covers proactive cost alerts for customers who want notice when
spend crosses a monthly threshold. It is useful for budget monitoring and early
warning, not for reviewing past invoices or requesting an adjustment after the
fact.

## What the customer provides

Support needs the account number, the threshold amount or percentage, and the
email contacts or distribution list that should receive the alert. Customers may
also choose whether they want one warning level or multiple levels such as 75
percent and 90 percent of expected spend.

## What support provides

Support confirms whether alerting is enabled on the managed account, records the
threshold values, and states when alerts are evaluated. In this corpus, standard
cost alerts are checked every six hours against accumulated managed-service
spend. Support can also explain how alert recipients are updated if team
ownership changes.

## Limits

Cost alerts do not recalculate charges, identify billing discrepancies, or
provide service-health notices. They are preventive controls only.
