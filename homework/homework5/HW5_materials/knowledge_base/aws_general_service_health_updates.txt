## Overview

Use this article for general questions about current platform status, broad
service health summaries, and unscheduled impact notices affecting AWS
Management Service. It is intended for customers asking whether the managed
platform is presently healthy, degraded, or recovering.

## What support provides

Support can summarize whether the service is operating normally, whether an
unscheduled incident is open, and whether any region-wide degradations are under
investigation. General health updates include the incident state, affected
components, and the time of the last published update.

## What the customer provides

Customers usually only need to provide the region or managed environment they
are asking about. If they report a symptom, a timestamp and short impact
description help support compare it with the live status feed.

## Limits

This article is not a scheduled-maintenance notice and does not provide the
scope, timing, or preparation steps for a named planned event. Planned
maintenance guidance is handled separately from general health reporting.
