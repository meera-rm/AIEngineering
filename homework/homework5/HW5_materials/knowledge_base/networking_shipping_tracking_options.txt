## Overview

Use this article when a customer asks how quickly networking equipment can be
delivered, which shipping methods are available, or whether a package can be
tracked after dispatch. It applies to stocked routers, switches, and approved
accessories shipped through the standard fulfillment program.

## Standard timelines

For in-stock hardware, standard ground delivery is quoted at five to seven
business days from order release. Expedited delivery is two to three business
days, and priority overnight service is available in eligible metro regions for
orders cleared before the daily cutoff. Delivery commitments begin after order
verification and payment approval, not when the cart is created or the inquiry
is sent.

## What the customer provides

To quote a realistic delivery window, support needs the destination region,
whether the order is shipping to a business or residential address, and whether
the customer wants standard, expedited, or priority service. The customer does
not need a tracking number before the order has shipped.

## What support provides

Support checks inventory status, confirms which shipping methods are available
for the destination, and explains the quoted transit range. Once the order is
dispatched, tracking details are normally posted to the order record within 24
hours and can also be sent by email on request. If multiple packages are used,
support explains which items are in each shipment.

Support should also explain that quoted delivery windows assume the order clears
verification before the daily cutoff. If stock must be moved between warehouses,
the customer should be told that dispatch timing may shift by one business day
even when the selected carrier service remains the same. For time-sensitive
projects, support can note whether split shipment is available so essential
hardware can leave first and accessories can follow.

## Limits

This article does not authorize shipping-address changes after carrier handoff
and does not define the return window if the product is unsuitable. Those are
separate processes with different requirements.
