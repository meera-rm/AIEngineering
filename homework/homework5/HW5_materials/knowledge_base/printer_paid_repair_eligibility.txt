## Overview

This article covers bench repair requests for printers that are outside warranty
or have damage excluded from warranty terms. It applies to customers who want an
estimate for repair service rather than an exchange review.

## What the customer provides

The customer should provide the model, serial number, age of the unit if known,
and a short description of the fault. Photos are useful when the request
involves impact damage, broken trays, jammed mechanical parts, or liquid
exposure. The customer is not expected to diagnose which assembly failed.

## What support provides

Support checks whether the model is still accepted for paid repair, quotes the
inspection fee, and explains the turnaround estimate for the service center. If
repair is approved, support issues the depot intake instructions and explains
that the final repair price is not confirmed until inspection is complete.

## Limits

This article does not determine eligibility for in-warranty exchange after
documented print-quality troubleshooting. It is also not meant for wireless
setup issues that can be resolved without hardware service.
