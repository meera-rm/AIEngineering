## Overview

Use this article after safe troubleshooting suggests a Samsung television may
need hands-on assessment for a display defect such as persistent flicker. The
standard television warranty in this corpus is a 12-month limited hardware
warranty beginning on the retail purchase date. For covered in-home models,
support routes the case to an authorized technician rather than promising an
immediate replacement.

## What the customer provides

The customer should provide the model, serial number, purchase date, seller
name, and proof of purchase. For intermittent display problems, a short photo or
video clip is helpful, along with notes about which sources were tested and
whether the built-in menu also flickers. The customer may also be asked to
confirm that the screen is free of impact cracks, liquid exposure, and
unauthorized repair attempts.

The customer is not expected to diagnose the failed part or to decide whether
the issue is covered.

## What support provides

Support checks warranty dates, validates symptom evidence, and determines
whether the case qualifies for technician dispatch or depot review. When the TV
is within warranty and the symptom remains after approved troubleshooting,
support opens a service order and offers the first available technician slot in
the customer's service region. If local dispatch is unavailable, support
explains the alternate inspection route before scheduling.

Before the visit, support should tell the customer to leave the television
assembled as currently installed and to keep the remote and proof of purchase
available. The technician decides whether the issue can be resolved on site or
whether a follow-up parts visit is needed. Support-owned decisions include the
service route, appointment window, and parts ordering; the customer only needs
to confirm access and contact availability.

## Coverage limits

Warranty routing is intended for hardware defects and persistent display
failures. Cosmetic damage, accidental breakage, liquid damage, and symptoms
caused only by unsupported accessories are excluded from standard warranty
service. Retail return windows are handled separately and are not part of this
article. Escalate only if warranty status is disputed or the customer cannot be
served by the normal authorized-service region.
