## Event summary

Maintenance event `AMS-2023-Q4-NET-17` is a scheduled AWS Management Service
network-control update planned for 18 November 2023 from 02:00 UTC to 04:00
UTC. The change window is expected to affect management-plane actions only. The
notice states that running customer workloads should remain online, but managed
scaling actions, configuration pushes, and health-status propagation may pause
briefly during the event.

## Expected impact

During the window, customers may see delayed dashboard status refreshes and up
to two short control-plane interruptions, each not expected to exceed 10
minutes. The event does not call for customer-side service restarts. If a
customer relies on just-in-time infrastructure changes during the window, those
changes should be rescheduled when possible.

## What support provides

Support should quote the event ID, planned start and end time, and the managed
service components affected. Updates are posted at event start, every 30
minutes while work remains active, and at completion. If the interruption
exceeds the notice threshold, support opens an incident bridge and posts an
unscheduled-impact update tied to the same event ID.
