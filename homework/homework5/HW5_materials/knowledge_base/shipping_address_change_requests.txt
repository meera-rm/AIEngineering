## Overview

Use this article when a customer wants to change the delivery address on an
existing hardware order. This process is separate from quoting shipping speed,
tracking a package, or explaining the return window.

## What the customer provides

Support needs the order number, the requested new address, and confirmation of
whether the order has already shipped. If the destination change affects tax or
export screening, the customer may also be asked to reconfirm billing details.

## What support provides

Support can update the address only before carrier handoff. Once the package has
been manifested and transferred to the carrier, the original address must remain
in place unless the carrier separately approves a reroute request. Support
should tell the customer whether the order is still editable and whether the
change will delay dispatch.

## Limits

This article does not estimate delivery timelines for a future purchase and does
not create tracking numbers before shipment.
