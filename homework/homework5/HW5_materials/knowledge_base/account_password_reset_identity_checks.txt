## Overview

Use this article when a customer cannot access an account and needs a password
reset. The focus is verifying identity safely before support changes login
credentials.

## What the customer provides

The customer should provide the account identifier, current contact email, and
one approved identity-check detail such as the last successful billing postcode
or the name of the authorized signer on file. Support should not ask for full
payment card numbers or plaintext passwords.

## What support provides

Support validates the identity check, issues the reset, and notes whether the
customer must update multifactor settings at the next sign-in. If the identity
record does not match, support routes the request to account recovery review.

## Limits

This article does not unlock suspended contracts, change billing contacts, or
override identity controls because the caller says access is urgent. Escalate
only when the customer no longer controls the registered email and standard
identity verification cannot be completed safely.
