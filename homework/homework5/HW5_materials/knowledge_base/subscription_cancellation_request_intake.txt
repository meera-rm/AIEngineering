## Overview

Use this article when a customer wants to cancel a recurring service subscription
and needs the request logged correctly. It is unrelated to hardware returns,
feature requests, consultations, or billing-discrepancy review.

## What the customer provides

The customer should provide the account or subscription number, the service they
want cancelled, and the requested effective date. If a contract term exists, the
customer may also be asked to confirm whether they want cancellation at term end
or an earlier reviewed exception.

## What support provides

Support confirms the subscription status, records the cancellation request,
explains the notice period that applies, and tells the customer whether any data
export or final billing steps remain before closure. Refund decisions, if any,
are handled under separate contract review rules.

## Limits

This article does not approve billing adjustments, cancel hardware orders in
transit, or act as a return authorization. If the customer is actually disputing
a charge or returning equipment, support should move to the correct policy
process.
