## Overview

Use this article when a Jira 8.20 customer needs help with who can browse,
create, transition, edit, or administer issues in a project. Permission-scheme
questions often sound similar to workflow requests but are controlled
independently.

## What the customer provides

The customer should identify the project, the affected users or groups, and the
action they cannot perform. Helpful evidence includes the exact screen where
access failed and whether the user recently changed roles.

## What support provides

Support checks the permission scheme, associated project roles, and whether the
problem is caused by missing membership or by a separate workflow condition.
Support may correct role assignments or permission mappings when the change is
approved.

The customer does not need to know the exact permission key names in advance.
They do need to describe the blocked action clearly so support can tell whether
the issue is access-related or part of the workflow design.

## Limits

This article does not tell the customer how to redesign statuses, transitions,
or priority levels. Those changes require separate workflow guidance.
