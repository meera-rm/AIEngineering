## Overview

This article supports Jira 8.20 requests to add statuses, modify transitions,
or align workflow conditions with a new ticket handling policy. It is commonly
retrieved alongside the custom priority article because teams often need both
sets of changes to reflect a revised issue process.

## What the customer provides

Support should ask for the current workflow name, the issue types affected, and
a simple before-and-after list of statuses and allowed transitions. If the
customer wants transitions to depend on priority, approval, or issue state, the
exact rule must be supplied in business language so it can be translated into
workflow conditions without guessing. A staging project or test window is also
important because workflow changes can affect active teams immediately.

The customer does not need to predefine every Jira configuration detail, but
they do need to state which statuses should be added, removed, or repurposed.

## What support provides

Support reviews the requested workflow map, checks for conflicts with existing
boards and issue screens, and advises whether the change fits native Jira 8.20
workflow capabilities. Standard support can help create new statuses, add or
restrict transitions, and apply clear transition conditions when the approval
rules are already defined. After configuration, support should validate that
issues can still move through the intended path and that no team loses the
ability to complete required work.

Once the before-and-after map and transition rules are complete, support
provides a change estimate. A single-project workflow with defined conditions
normally requires four business days for staging configuration and validation.
Shared workflows or dynamic rules require specialist review before a timeline
or plugin need can be confirmed. These are support-owned conclusions, not
details the customer is expected to supply.

## Dynamic behavior and limits

When a customer asks for issue handling to shift dynamically as project goals
change, support should narrow the request to explicit rules rather than vague
optimization. Jira can enforce named statuses and documented transition
conditions, but it cannot infer business priority without a defined policy
matrix. This article does not cover notification changes or permission-scheme
design. Escalate when multiple projects share one workflow and the requested
change would affect teams outside the customer's approved scope.
